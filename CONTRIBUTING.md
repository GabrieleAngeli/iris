# Contributing to Iris ICP

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Iris ICP.

By participating in this project, you agree to follow the contribution and
licensing requirements described below.

## License

Iris ICP is distributed under the MIT License.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions accepted into the project
will be distributed as part of Iris ICP under the MIT License.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before a contribution can be accepted, the contributor must agree to the
Contributor License Agreement contained in `CLA.md`.

The CLA does not prevent contributors from retaining ownership of their
contributions.

It grants the project maintainer the additional rights required to maintain,
distribute, sublicense, commercialize, and potentially relicense the project
in the future.

## Developer Certificate of Origin

Every contribution must comply with the Developer Certificate of Origin
(DCO).

By signing off a commit, the contributor certifies that they have the right
to submit the contribution to the project.

Commits must therefore contain a `Signed-off-by` line.

You can create a signed-off commit using:

    git commit -s -m "Your commit message"

Example:

    Signed-off-by: John Doe <john@example.com>

Pull requests containing commits that are not properly signed off may be
rejected.

## Contribution requirements

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that:

1. You created the contribution or otherwise have the legal right to submit it.
2. The contribution does not knowingly infringe third-party intellectual
   property rights.
3. You agree to license the contribution according to the project license.
4. You agree to the Contributor License Agreement.
5. You understand that accepted contributions may become part of commercial,
   hosted, proprietary, or differently licensed versions of Iris ICP.

## Pull Requests

Contributions should be submitted through pull requests.

Pull requests should:

- describe the purpose of the change;
- reference the related issue when applicable;
- include appropriate tests;
- preserve the architectural principles of the project;
- pass the project's automated checks;
- contain properly signed-off commits.

## Attribution

Contributors may be acknowledged in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`.

Copyright in individual contributions remains with their respective authors
unless a separate written copyright assignment agreement is executed.
